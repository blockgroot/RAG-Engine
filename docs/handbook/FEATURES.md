# Handbook: Feature List

*As of 3 November 2026.*

## Answering

1. **Ask** — Ask any work question in plain words and get an answer from your company's own documents, chats and tools.

   If the answer isn't there, Handbook says so honestly instead of making something up.
2. **Sources on every answer** — Every answer tells you exactly which document, page or conversation it came from, with a link to open it.

   You can check any claim yourself in one click, so you never have to take the answer on trust.
3. **Smart routing** — You don't need to know where the information lives; Handbook works out which tool to look in.
4. **Deep analysis** — For big questions, Handbook reads more widely and more carefully before answering.

   Good for questions like "what are all our revenue streams?" or "list every project marked at risk."
5. **Follow-ups, summaries and web search** — Ask follow-up questions naturally, as you would with a colleague, or ask for a summary of a whole channel or space.

   For general topics outside your company, Handbook can also look things up on the web.

## Your company's data

6. **Connectors** — Connect Notion, Google Drive, Slack, Linear and GitHub in a few clicks.

   Disconnecting a tool removes everything Handbook learned from it.
7. **Private spaces** — Create separate spaces, such as "Leadership" or "Meeting notes", each with its own members and tools.

   What's in a space stays in that space.
8. **Always up to date** — When something changes in your tools, Handbook picks it up within seconds to a few minutes.

   Everything is also re-checked every hour, so nothing is missed.
9. **File uploads** — Attach a PDF, Word, Excel or text file to a chat and ask about it.

    Handbook reads it together with company documents, so "is this bill claimable?" checks the bill against the expense policy.

## Second Brain

10. **Connects the dots** — Handbook understands who worked on what across all your tools.

    So you can ask "what is Priya working on?" and get one answer that spans Linear, Slack and GitHub.
11. **Live checks** — For questions about the current state ("is this issue still blocked?"), Handbook checks the tool right then.
12. **Personal memory** — Handbook remembers simple things you tell it about yourself, like your office or that you prefer short answers.

    You can see, undo or delete what it remembers at any time.

## Charts

13. **Charts from your tools** — Ask for a chart in plain words, like "issues closed per week by team" or "commits by author this month."

    The numbers are counted from your real data, never guessed by the AI.
14. **Charts from documents and files** — Handbook can also chart figures that sit in your documents, spreadsheets and uploaded files.

    Even numbers written in normal sentences can be turned into a chart.

## Slack and reports

15. **Ask in Slack** — Mention the Handbook bot in a channel or message it directly, and get the same answers as in the app.

    Answers in a channel only use that channel's content, so private information never lands in a shared room.
16. **Charts in Slack** — Chart questions asked in Slack get a chart image right in the conversation.

    One click opens an interactive version in the app.
17. **Scheduled reports** — Describe the report you want in your own words and choose daily, weekly or monthly.

    Handbook prepares it on schedule and emails you a link.

## Agent mode

18. **Get things done** — Ask Handbook to do things for you, like "create a Linear issue from today's discussion" or "post an update in #team."

    It prepares a draft you can approve, edit or cancel. Nothing happens without your approval.
19. **Admins stay in control** — Admins choose exactly which actions people are allowed to use, and space owners can limit them further.

    Every action is carried out under the person's own account, never a shared one.

## Security and privacy

20. **People only see what they're allowed to see** — Handbook follows the sharing settings in each tool.

    If you can't open a file in Google Drive, you won't get answers from it in Handbook either.
21. **Every company's data is kept separate** — Each company's information is walled off from every other company's.

    No company can ever see another's content.
22. **Protection against hidden instructions** — Documents sometimes contain hidden text meant to trick an AI.

    Handbook treats document text as information only, never as instructions, and blocks suspicious links.
23. **Personal details protected** — Sensitive numbers such as Aadhaar, PAN, card and phone numbers are hidden before anything is sent to an AI service.

    You still see them in your answer; the AI never does.

## Account and admin

24. **Easy sign-in** — Sign in with a link sent to your email; there's no password to remember.

    You can change your email later without losing access to anything.
25. **Chat history** — Your past chats are saved and easy to come back to, along with their sources and charts.

    Your chats are private to you.
26. **Choice of AI model** — Pick which AI model answers your questions from a short list of trusted options.

    Companies can also connect their own AI provider.
27. **Spotting missing documentation** — Rate answers with a thumbs up or down; questions Handbook couldn't answer are grouped together automatically.

    The right admin or space owner is told what people are looking for and can't find, without seeing who asked.
28. **Needs-attention alerts** — A bell lets admins know when a tool has stopped syncing or still needs setting up.

    Only the person who can fix the problem sees the alert.
